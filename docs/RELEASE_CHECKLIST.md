Product-behavior and local release-source baseline: 0.1.0, 2026-07-18.

The 0.1.0 product-behavior backlog, release-source/evidence work through NCT-085, native hardening through NCT-102 plus NCT-109/NCT-116 through NCT-119, NCT-120 compact logical history, NCT-104 daily-editing workflows, and NCT-105 bounded grammar conversion are implemented; NCT-104/NCT-105/NCT-120 are complete, while NCT-106/NCT-110/NCT-111/NCT-112/NCT-119/NCT-121/NCT-122 remain partial at their retained acceptance boundaries. NCT-110 retains bounded headless installed Codex login/Vim/btop, nano/less/dialog, and top/watch/info/whiptail/fzf/sqlite3/glances/bash/man/sftp PTY/VT tranches, not authenticated or mapped compositor proof. NoctEd's standalone foundation, the NCT-106 state-recovery/legacy-parser/fuzz-race and allocator-accounted core-stability tranches, and the NCT-121/NCT-122 immutable-index, external-change, two-lane, and streaming-search slices are implemented. NCT-121 retains synthetic 4K/25K/100K index/delta/model-memory plus bounded X11/Wayland current-project tree-draw, actual monitor-burst/publication, overflow-recovery, and post-widget callback evidence. NCT-122 retains a 128-cycle-per-mode three-query deterministic-ranking fingerprint with zero allocator residual and root memory samples plus two eight-cycle native Wayland Debug runs through GTK search publication/draw, cancellation, open/save-under-load, deterministic Quick Open, bounded batches, and drain. NCT-106/NCT-121/NCT-122 remain partial pending long native-GUI soaks, sustained lifecycle proof, and matching `ReleaseSafe`/X11 search-load, peak process-tree-memory, and long-duration cross-backend distributions. This checklist keeps local source/native evidence separate from mature-editor parity and from credentialed, retained-artifact/signature, publication, and consent-gated operations.

NCT-100 reproducible split-terminal benchmarking, NCT-101 throughput hardening, NCT-102 event-driven PTY scheduling, NCT-118 short resource qualification, and NCT-120 history qualification are complete machine-local engineering lanes; they do not satisfy any retained release-operation row.
